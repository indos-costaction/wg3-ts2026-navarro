IC Trainer

Classifying EEG independent components for artifact correction

Simulated components for teaching: topographies on a 91-channel montage + 4 EOG, 100 trials from −500 to 1000 ms at 128 Hz. Measures follow SASICA’s logic (adaptive threshold: dataset mean ± 2 SD, 4 SD for EOG correlations). Based on Chaumon M, Bishop DVM, Busch NA (2015). A practical guide to the selection of independent components of the electroencephalogram for artifact correction. J Neurosci Methods 250:47–63. doi:10.1016/j.jneumeth.2015.02.025
